Title: Sci-Fi Flash Fiction
Model: gemini-3-pro-preview (Google Gemini 3 family, hosted API)
Prompt: Write a 150-word flash fiction story about an astronaut who discovers a garden growing inside an asteroid. End with a twist.
---
Commander Diaz drilled for ice and hit soil. Inside asteroid KA-9, her headlamp found rows of green: tomatoes, marigolds, beans climbing strings tied to pitons, all breathing in recycled dark. No station, no log, just a hand-painted sign: WATER ME — J.

For three days she tended it, rationing her own water, wondering which lost mission had left a gardener. The leaves turned toward her lamp as if grateful. On the fourth morning, the beans had spelled a new word overnight in their curling vines: STAY.

Her radio crackled then — Mission Control, frantic: "Diaz, you've been silent 72 hours. Your oxygen should have run out on day one."

She looked at her gauge. Full. She looked at the vines, now gently wrapping her boots, the tomatoes warm as skin. The sign had changed. It read: WATERED — J. And she finally understood whose initials were carved into her own helmet.
