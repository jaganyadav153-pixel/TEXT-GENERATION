Prompt ID: P22
Title: Counterfactual Reasoning
Model: gemini-3-pro-preview (Google Gemini 3 family, hosted API)
Prompt: In 120 words, speculate: What if smartphones had never been invented? Describe one positive and one negative societal outcome.
---
Without smartphones, we'd still carry separate cameras, maps, and MP3 players, tethered to desktops for internet.

Positive: Public spaces might be more present. No doomscrolling at dinners; boredom could revive daydreaming, street games, and long conversations. Sleep and attention spans would likely be healthier, and small talk with strangers more common.

Negative: Massive loss of access. A farmer checking crop prices, a migrant worker video-calling home, a student in a village attending online classes — all depend on cheap pocket internet. Banking, emergency help, and gig work for millions would be slower and more unequal.

We'd be less distracted, but also less connected when it matters most.
