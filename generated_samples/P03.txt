Title: Poem - Nature
Model: gpt-5 (OpenAI GPT-5 family, hosted API)
Prompt: Write a 12-line free-verse poem about monsoon rains in a city, focusing on sound and memory.
---
First it drums on tin awnings like impatient fingers,
then the gutters learn to sing.
Buses hiss past, throwing up silver curtains,
and every balcony becomes a small, wet orchestra.

I remember grandmother calling the clothes in,
her voice racing the thunder,
the smell of earth rising to meet us
like an old friend who never writes but always arrives.

Now I stand at this window, city blurring to watercolor,
traffic lights bleeding red and gold,
and let the rain translate everything I miss
into a language I can almost understand.
