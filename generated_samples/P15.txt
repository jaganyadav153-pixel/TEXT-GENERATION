Prompt ID: P15
Title: Dialogue Writing
Model: gpt-5 (OpenAI GPT-5 family, hosted API)
Prompt: Write a natural 10-line dialogue between a job applicant and an interviewer where the applicant asks about work-life balance.
---
Interviewer: So, do you have any questions for us?
Applicant: Yes — how does the team usually handle work-life balance during release weeks?
Interviewer: Honest answer? Releases get intense, but we rota on-call and discourage weekend pings.
Applicant: That's good to hear. Are flexible hours an option when there's no deadline?
Interviewer: Absolutely. Most of us do 10 to 4 core hours, rest async.
Applicant: And vacation — is it actually encouraged?
Interviewer: Yes, managers nudge you if you haven't taken time off in a quarter.
Applicant: Perfect. I do my best work when I can recharge fully.
Interviewer: That's exactly the culture we're trying to protect.
