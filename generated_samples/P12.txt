Prompt: In 150 words, explain why the printing press was transformative for Europe in the 15th century. Mention two specific effects.
---
Before Gutenberg's press around 1440, books were hand-copied by monks — slow, expensive, and rare. The movable-type press could print 3,000 pages a day, cutting book costs by over 80%.

Two effects changed Europe. First, knowledge spread: Luther's 95 Theses were reprinted and shared across Germany within weeks in 1517, fueling the Reformation — impossible in a manuscript age. Second, science accelerated: standard identical textbooks and tables let scholars in Venice and Krakow compare the same data, correct errors, and build on each other, laying groundwork for the Scientific Revolution.

Literacy rose from under 10% toward 20%+ in towns within a century. Ideas were no longer locked in monasteries; they traveled with merchants, students, and reformers.
