Prompt: Explain quantum computing to a 10-year-old in under 120 words using a simple analogy. No jargon without explanation.
---
Imagine a normal computer is like flipping one coin at a time — heads or tails, one answer at a time.

A quantum computer is like spinning many coins at once. While spinning, each coin is a blurry mix of heads AND tails. Scientists call this blurry state a qubit.

Because it holds many possibilities together, it can try lots of paths at once — like exploring every hallway of a maze simultaneously instead of one by one.

We then stop the spinning and read the best answer. It's not magic, just clever physics that helps with big puzzles like medicines and secret codes.
