Prompt ID: P04
Title: Haiku Chain
Model: gemini-3-pro-preview (Google Gemini 3 family, hosted API)
Prompt: Write three linked haikus (5-7-5 each) about sunrise, noon, and sunset over a desert.
---
Sunrise:
pale gold spills softly —
lizard tracks write morning news
on cool, rippled sand.

Noon:
white sun hammers down,
no shadow dares to linger,
stones hold their hot breath.

Sunset:
violet dunes bleed —
a coyote's lone prayer fades
into star-salt night.
